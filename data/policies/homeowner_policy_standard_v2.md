# Homeowner Policy Standard — Version 2.0

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Document Type:** Policy  
**Department:** Underwriting

---

## Section A: Dwelling Coverage

### Clause A-1: Covered Perils
We cover risk of direct physical loss to property described in Coverage A (Dwelling) and B (Other Structures) except for losses excluded in Section C (Exclusions). Water damage resulting from sudden and accidental discharge from a plumbing system (including burst pipes) is covered. Gradual leakage or seepage over 14 days or more is not covered.

### Clause A-2: Coverage Limits
The limit of liability for Coverage A (Dwelling) shown on the declarations page is the maximum we will pay for a covered loss. Coverage B (Other Structures) is limited to 10% of the Coverage A limit. You must pay the deductible shown on the declarations page before we pay any claim.

### Clause A-3: Deductible
All covered claims are subject to the deductible amount stated in your declarations. The deductible applies once per claim, not per item. For wind/hail claims in certain regions, a separate percentage deductible may apply.

---

## Section B: Personal Property

### Clause B-1: Personal Property Coverage
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured anywhere in the world. The limit for personal property is 50% of the Coverage A limit unless a different amount is shown on the declarations. Certain items such as jewelry, silverware, and fine arts have sub-limits (e.g., $1,500 for jewelry).

### Clause B-2: Actual Cash Value
We will pay the actual cash value at the time of loss for personal property, but not more than the amount required to repair or replace the property. Actual cash value means replacement cost minus depreciation.

---

## Section C: Exclusions

### Clause C-1: Flood Exclusion
We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these, whether driven by wind or not. Flood insurance may be purchased separately through the NFIP.

### Clause C-2: Wear and Tear
We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, rust, mold, or inherent vice. We do not cover loss caused by neglect (failure to use reasonable means to save property).

### Clause C-3: Earth Movement
We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, landslide, mudslide, or other earth movement. Earthquake coverage may be available by endorsement.

---

## Section D: Claim Filing Requirements

### Clause D-1: Notice and Proof
You must give us prompt notice of a loss. You must submit a signed, sworn proof of loss within 60 days of our request. You must cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of the claim.

### Clause D-2: Appraisal
If we and you disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. Each party will select a competent appraiser; the two appraisers will select an umpire. A decision agreed to by any two will set the amount of loss.
