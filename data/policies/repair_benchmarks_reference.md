# Repair Cost Benchmarks Reference

**Document Type:** Reference  
**Department:** Claims  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15

---

## Water Damage

### Minor (Single Room)
- **Description:** Small leak, one room affected, minimal drywall/flooring.
- **Cost Range (USD):** $1,200 – $2,500
- **Typical scope:** Extract water, dry, minor repair, paint.
- **Region:** National average; coastal and high-cost areas +15–25%.

### Moderate (Multiple Rooms)
- **Description:** Burst pipe or appliance leak affecting multiple rooms.
- **Cost Range (USD):** $2,500 – $5,000
- **Typical scope:** Extraction, drying, drywall repair, flooring, paint, possible mold remediation.
- **Region:** National average.

### Severe (Whole House / Structural)
- **Description:** Major flooding, structural involvement, or prolonged exposure.
- **Cost Range (USD):** $8,000 – $25,000+
- **Typical scope:** Full remediation, structural repair, full replacement of affected areas.
- **Region:** Varies by square footage and region.

## Fire / Smoke Damage

### Minor
- **Cost Range (USD):** $2,000 – $5,000
- **Scope:** One room, cleaning, repaint, minor repairs.

### Moderate
- **Cost Range (USD):** $5,000 – $15,000
- **Scope:** Multiple rooms, HVAC cleaning, content cleaning.

### Severe
- **Cost Range (USD):** $20,000 – $100,000+
- **Scope:** Structural repair, full rebuild of affected areas.

---

## Roof Repair

### Minor (Shingle Repair)
- **Cost Range (USD):** $350 – $1,200
- **Scope:** Up to 100 sq ft, shingle replacement.

### Moderate (Partial Replacement)
- **Cost Range (USD):** $3,000 – $7,000
- **Scope:** One or two slopes, flashing, underlayment.

### Full Replacement
- **Cost Range (USD):** $8,000 – $25,000
- **Scope:** Full roof, varies by size and material.

---

*Use these benchmarks for estimate validation only. Actual costs depend on region, contractor, and specific scope.*
